# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes respectful, evidence-aware collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Debate decisions and evidence without attacking people.
- Preserve human authority, material dissent, provenance, and uncertainty.
- Distinguish validated facts, inferences, configured checks, and unverified outcomes.
- Protect private studio state, credentials, personal data, and confidential product information.
- Give specific feedback and credit sources and contributors.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate deception, and publishing another person's confidential material are not acceptable.

For a concern that is safe to discuss publicly, open an Issue with only the minimum necessary detail. For a sensitive concern, use a private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile and do not disclose details publicly. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may restrict participation.
